About Qiushi Xie

Education, research interests, and selected work

All WorkPapersCompetitionsSide ProjectsAbout

I am Qiushi Xie (谢秋实), a final-year undergraduate in Communication Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). I have been admitted through the recommendation route to the College of Information Science and Electronic Engineering at Zhejiang University, with direct-entry PhD studies planned to begin in 2027.

My research interests include trustworthy AI question answering, clustered metasurfaces, intelligent information systems, and embedded development. I am interested in evidence-grounded, verifiable answers in real-world intelligent systems, and in connecting communication and sensing with algorithms and practical hardware and software.

As a remote undergraduate research assistant advised by Hao Tang, I am a co-first author of VaseMuseum, a digital intelligent museum for ancient Greek pottery. The work is available as an arXiv preprint and has been submitted to Nature Machine Intelligence. My lithium-ion battery remaining-useful-life estimation paper was published in the SPIE conference proceedings in 2026; I am its sole author.

I led the multimodal drone ground-station project in the Intel Cup, which received a National Second Prize and placed in the top 7.83%. I also led the digital-key system project that received a First Prize in the Hubei division of the National Undergraduate Electronics Design Contest. My personal projects include Codex Tidy, Boring Notch Focus, and a multi-display window-switching tool for macOS.

This site documents my research papers, competition projects, and personal work, with links to publications, code, and project pages. The Chinese entrance is qiushi0919.cn, and the English entrance is qiushi0919.github.io. Research and project enquiries are welcome by email.
