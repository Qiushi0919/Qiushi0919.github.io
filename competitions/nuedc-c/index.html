Wireless Digital Key Experimental System

Qiushi Xie‡, Tuoyuan Zhu, Yachen Pang, Jingyuan Xia*, Kan Xiao*

Jul–Aug 2026 · 2026 National Undergraduate Electronics Design Contest (Hubei) · Hubei First Prize · HUST's only first-prize team for this problem

A digital key system using UWB ToF ranging, four-antenna PDoA angle estimation, and Kalman filtering for 4-bit identity verification, real-time three-zone classification, audiovisual greeting, and automatic locking control.

The 2026 contest was held at the provincial level with no national round; First Prize in the Hubei Division was the highest award available that year.
